Why I made this

Study Tracker is a small study tool for practicing English tenses. It covers the 8 main tenses, with a short explanation of the structure, a few example sentences, and a quiz for each one so you can check what you remember.

This was made as a school project to practice building a website with login, several pages, and content that changes depending on what you pick.
